RYX Support

Stuck, curious, or something not working? We want to hear about it.

Contact us

Email info@leeamapplications.com and a human will reply, normally within two working days. Tell us what phone you use and what happened, and screenshots help if you have them.

Quick answers

Before emailing, the frequently asked questions on our home page cover the common ones: which calendars RYX syncs with, how the free plan works, and how voice data is handled.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google. To cancel, use the subscription settings on your phone: on iPhone, Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions; on Android, the Play Store, then your profile, then Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal and your plan runs to the end of the paid period.

Delete your account

See the Delete Your Account page for the two ways to do this, one instant in the app, one by email.

Accessibility

RYX and this website are built to work properly with screen readers, and we treat accessibility problems as bugs, not feature requests. If anything in the app or on this site does not work well with VoiceOver, TalkBack or any other assistive technology, email us and it goes to the top of the list.
